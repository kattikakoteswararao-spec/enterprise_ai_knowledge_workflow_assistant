1. Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted to the manager for approval.


2. Work From Home
Employees may request work from home with manager approval.
Employees must follow company security policies while working remotely.


3. Password Security
Employees must use strong passwords and must never share passwords.
Passwords must be kept confidential.


4. Security Incidents
Employees must report suspected security incidents to the IT security team immediately.


5. Training
Employees must complete the mandatory security awareness training every year.
